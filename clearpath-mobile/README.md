# ClearPath

ClearPath is an original, iOS-first wellbeing prototype for people who want a calmer, more intentional relationship with their screen habits and who are stepping away from compulsive adult-content use. It is a private reflection tool, not a medical service or a device-level content blocker.

## What is included

| Area | Included experience |
|---|---|
| Today | A private day count, supportive prompt, and short readiness check-in. |
| Reset | A three-step pause with grounding guidance and healthy next-action choices. |
| Journey | A local seven-day activity view, check-in history, and reset count. |
| Plan | An editable reason, challenging moment, and replacement action. |
| Profile & Privacy | Plain-language local-only storage notice, care guidance, and a data-clear control. |

ClearPath stores data locally through AsyncStorage. The prototype includes no account, cloud sync, advertising, social feed, direct messaging, AI chat, payment feature, or access to browsing history.

## Run locally

Install dependencies with `pnpm install`, then start the development service with `pnpm dev`. Use `pnpm check` for a TypeScript check and `pnpm test` for the unit suite.

## Product boundaries

The app avoids shame-based language and does not treat a lapse as failure. It includes no explicit content and does not claim to diagnose, treat, or cure a health condition. Users who feel unsafe or unable to cope should reach out to a trusted adult or qualified professional.
